Before the modules

Conversation Journal

This is a place to catch what you are thinking while you move through Entrepalooza, especially the thoughts that do not fit neatly inside a workbook question.

You do not need to journal well.

A sentence is enough. A question is enough. “I don't know yet” is enough. You can use this page often, once, or not at all.

What it is

A running place for questions, observations, ideas, reactions, things you want to try, and choices you are considering.

What it isn't

It is not homework, a diary you have to maintain, a test, therapy, or something the program scores or interprets for you.

How to use it

Write when something feels worth remembering. You can come here from any module, add a rough note, then go back to what you were doing.
