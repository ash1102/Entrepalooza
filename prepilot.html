Entrepalooza

You don't need the whole plan.

Start with something worth exploring.

Entrepalooza is a guided learning experience for adults exploring what comes next in work and life. Notice what interests you, try something manageable, and use what you learn to choose your next step.

A small first pilot

We're developing the first Entrepalooza pilot.

We're planning for approximately 4-6 adults, with guided online sessions currently planned.

The opening sequence explores your interests, the conditions that help you work, and ways to keep effort manageable.

Dates, time commitment, and cost are still being finalized. Those details will be shared before you decide whether to join.

Walk through the pilot

You choose how you participate.

You can contribute, write privately, observe, or pass. You don't need to share your personal history, pitch a business, or choose a permanent direction.

Entrepalooza is educational. It is not therapy or crisis support and does not guarantee employment, income, or business success.
