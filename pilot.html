Walk through Pilot 1

Pilot 1 opens Entrepalooza, a small, human-guided cohort in Houston for adults figuring out what comes next. Here's how it feels, one step at a time.

Enter with Agency

01 Notice

Ground rules come first: what this is and isn't, your privacy, and your right to pass or leave.

See the 5 lesson steps
  1. What this pilot is and isn’t
  2. Rights, privacy, and separate consent
  3. Group Code and permission-based feedback
  4. Participation and Support Plan
  5. Your participation decision and handoff
02 Try

Practice feedback with permission. In a made-up scenario, a peer asks, “Do you want a question, a suggestion, or no feedback?” You decide, and you can say no.

03 Reflect

What would make taking part workable for you?

04 Decide

Just for you. Nothing you type is saved or sent.

Find Something Worth Exploring

01 Notice

Notice what holds your attention, or start with “I don't know yet.” You don't need a passion or a business idea.

See the 5 lesson steps
  1. Notice without forcing an answer
  2. Patterns of engagement (not types of people)
  3. Choose an interest or try something new
  4. Reflection, evidence, conditions, and cost
  5. Review, next decision, and handoff
02 Try

Pick an interest you already have, or something new. Design one small try with a limit and a stop rule.

03 Reflect

What caught your attention? What did it cost? Would you go back to it?

04 Decide

Just for you. Nothing you type is saved or sent.

Test How I Operate

Test How I Operate

01 Notice

Look at how you work, not who you “are.” No personality scores.

See the 3 lesson steps
  1. Understand the setup around a task
  2. Run one changed-condition test
  3. Review the cost and carry evidence forward
02 Try

Take an ordinary task and change one condition, like time of day, working alone or with people, or quiet or busy.

03 Reflect

What changed? What did it cost? What's still uncertain?

04 Decide

Just for you. Nothing you type is saved or sent.

Check the Story
No confession required.
Make room between event and verdict

Check the Story

01 Notice

Separate what happened from the story about it, and from any verdict about yourself.

See the 3 lesson steps
  1. Separate event, story, and verdict
  2. Choose one standard and observable practice
  3. Review, revise, and choose the next behavior
02 Try

With a made-up example, sort three things: the event, the story, and the verdict.

Event: A person shared a sketch with a peer; there was no reply that day.

Story: “They didn't like it.”

Verdict: “I'm not capable.”

03 Reflect

Is there a wording that's more accurate, not just more positive?

04 Decide

Just for you. Nothing you type is saved or sent.

Navigate Intensity
Pilot 1 ends here.
Keep your limits in the picture

Navigate Intensity

01 Notice

Sometimes effort spikes, stalls, or costs too much. Sometimes something is already working.

See the 3 lesson steps
  1. Notice signal, story, urge, and cost
  2. Protect what works or try one small response
  3. Check intuition and close Pilot 1
02 Try

Choose a route. Both are equal: protect one condition that's working, or try one small, low-risk response during an ordinary task.

03 Reflect

What's one limit you choose to keep?

04 Decide

Just for you. Nothing you type is saved or sent.
