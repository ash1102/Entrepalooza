Lesson 1 of 5·Try it here

What this pilot is and isn’t

Know what the pilot will and won’t do before choosing anything.

Learn

You can answer aloud, write privately, observe, or pass. Passing never needs a reason. Entrepalooza is a structured way to notice, try something small, reflect, and decide what comes next. A job, training, a project, a pause, or still looking can all be complete finishes.

Lesson 2 of 5·Try it here

Obsessive and harmonious patterns, not labels

Describe how an activity is operating without turning the pattern into a type of person.

More chosen / flexible

Can pause, adjust, return, and fit the activity beside other responsibilities.

More pressured / rigid

Hard to stop or adjust, and pressure or self-worth starts controlling the activity.

What do you notice?

Someone loves making music. This week they can pause for dinner, change the plan when tired, and return tomorrow.

The passion research is used as a noticing lens, not a label or diagnosis.

Lesson 1 of 3·Try it here

Understand the setup around a task

Describe one condition around a task without turning it into a personality score.

Learn

An operating condition can include instruction format, noise, company, choice, time, uncertainty, access, or support. The same task can feel different when one condition changes.

Same task, different setup

Task: alphabetize cup, book, apple.

Paragraph

Read the three words, alphabetize them, then check all three are included.

Checklist

1. Read. 2. Alphabetize. 3. Check all three.

One attempt can raise a useful question. It cannot prove a permanent preference.

Lesson 2 of 3·Try it here

Protect what works or try one small response

Continuation and navigation are equal routes. You do not need a setback to complete this lesson.

Choose a route

Continuation: What condition is supporting choice and flexibility? What boundary do you want to protect? Continue, deepen, or repeat are all valid.
Navigation: Clarify an instruction, reduce scope, add a stopping point, ask for support, change the environment, or stop safely. Useful does not have to mean calm.

Reflect

Was the next choice easier, harder, unchanged, or unclear? What effort or after-effect appeared? What else might explain the result?
